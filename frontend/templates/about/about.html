Platform Overview

Medication Safety, Simplified

MediLens turns physical medicine packaging into verified, accessible healthcare intelligence. Combining computer vision with Gemini AI, we eliminate dosage ambiguity, track shelf inventories, and safeguard patients and caregivers against medication errors.

Core Architecture

Three integrated systems designed for patient safety and low cognitive load.

Computer Vision OCR

Preprocesses strip packaging with OpenCV and performs multi-layer optical recognition with EasyOCR to extract active salts, batch codes, and expiration dates.

Gemini Clinical AI

Translates dense pharmaceutical jargon into clear, patient-friendly guidance, highlighting precautions, contraindications, and authenticity indicators.

Caregiver Command

Maintains an updated medicine shelf with automated expiry monitors, daily dose alarms, and instant emergency protocols to prevent missed or duplicate doses.
